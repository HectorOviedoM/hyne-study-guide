# Hyne Study Guide

Personal English study site from notes on Norman J. Hyne, *Nontechnical Guide to Petroleum Geology, Exploration, Drilling & Production* (3rd ed.).

**Chapters:** Introduction · 1 · 9 · 10 · 11 · 14 · 15 · 16 · 17 · 18

Open `index.html` locally. Diagrams are original. Not a substitute for the book.

## How to read

1. Open `index.html` in a browser.
2. Use the search box and chapter cards.
3. Teal **Data engineer / WITSML** boxes map terms to typical objects and columns.
